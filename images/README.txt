==========================================================================
                 PORTFOLIO IMAGE DROP-IN GUIDE
==========================================================================

Welcome! This folder holds the photos for your personal portfolio website.
The site is built with beautiful CSS gradient fallbacks, so it looks stunning
right out of the box even before you add your photos.

Whenever you want to show your own photos, simply copy your image files into
this "images/" folder with the exact filenames listed below.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PROFILE PHOTO (About Page - "The Traveller")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Filename:         profile.jpg
Where it appears: about.html (framed inside a mountain-shaped silhouette)
Recommended size: 800 x 1000 px (4:5 vertical portrait aspect ratio)
Tips:             A photo of you standing in the mountains, near your car,
                  or a clear friendly portrait with outdoor lighting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROJECT COVER IMAGES (Projects Page - "Expeditions")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Filename:         project-rupeewise.jpg
Where it appears: RupeeWise card in index.html & projects.html
Recommended size: 1200 x 750 px (16:10 aspect ratio)
Tips:             Screenshot of the Android app running, mockups of the
                  Khaata screen or vault lock screen.

Filename:         project-gatisetu.jpg
Where it appears: GatiSetu card in index.html & projects.html
Recommended size: 1200 x 750 px (16:10 aspect ratio)
Tips:             Screenshot of the logistics shipper dashboard or
                  NH-48 corridor truck tracking map.

Filename:         project3.jpg  &  project4.jpg
Where it appears: Placeholder projects in projects.html
Recommended size: 1200 x 750 px

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. OFF-ROAD PHOTO GALLERIES (Interests Page - "Off-Road")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mountains & Trekking:
  - trek1.jpg   (High Himalayan pass, e.g. Khardung La / Rohtang / Spiti)
  - trek2.jpg   (Campfire under the night sky or mountain trail)
  - trek3.jpg   (Summit view or rocky ridge)

Cars & Driving:
  - drive1.jpg  (Car on an open highway or twisting ghat road)
  - drive2.jpg  (Night drive cockpit view / dashboard dials)
  - drive3.jpg  (Parked car on a cliffside during sunset or twilight)

Travel & Road Trips:
  - trip1.jpg   (Scenic winding highway route)
  - trip2.jpg   (Milestone signboard or mountain road turn)
  - trip3.jpg   (Stargazing / night landscape)

Recommended size for gallery photos: 1200 x 800 px (3:2 or 16:9 ratio)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. OPTIONAL BACKGROUND BANNERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - band-mountains.jpg  (Full-width banner for Mountains section)
  - band-cars.jpg       (Full-width banner for Cars section)
  - band-trips.jpg      (Full-width banner for Road Trips section)
  - hero-bg.jpg         (Optional custom dusk mountain backdrop)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. RESUME PDF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Place your "resume.pdf" in the MAIN ROOT directory (not inside images/):
  portfolio/resume.pdf
The "Download Resume" buttons on the About and Contact pages will link
directly to this file.
