Navigating Complex Terrain, In Code & On The Road

Based out of Gujarat, India, I am a B.Tech Computer Science student with a passionate focus on native Android development, systems architecture, and distributed problem-solving. My obsession with software started with a desire to build tools that work completely without friction: zero latency, zero unnecessary network requests, and absolute reliability.

I specialize in crafting offline-first native applications with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room SQLite, and reactive state architectures. From developing secure on-device biometric fintech vaults like RupeeWise to designing corridor-wide B2B logistics ecosystems like GatiSetu, I love turning convoluted real-world bottlenecks into sleek, resilient interfaces.

When I am not sitting before an IDE, you will find me behind the wheel on an open highway or trekking through high-altitude Himalayan passes. Driving taught me that unexpected detours require calm decision-making; mountaineering taught me that endurance and preparation beat quick shortcuts. This mindset directly reflects in my code: modular, rigorously structured, and built to survive edge cases.
