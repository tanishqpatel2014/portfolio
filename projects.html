Native Android // Offline-First Fintech

RupeeWise

On-Device Privacy · Khaata Debt Ledger · Group Bill Splitting · Biometric Vault

The Challenge & The Problem

Most modern personal finance and expense-tracking applications require continuous cloud sync, read confidential SMS messages without transparency, and leak intimate financial metadata to third-party ad networks. Furthermore, traditional Khaata (debt ledger) apps do not accommodate granular partial repayment installments or group splitting without requiring all friends to sign up for accounts.

The Architecture & Solution

RupeeWise is engineered as a 100% offline-first native Android application. It functions with zero internet permission, meaning user data literally cannot leave the physical handset. All data is structured within a normalized Room SQLite database.

  • 3-in-1 Financial Core: Unified daily expense manager, Khaata (borrow/lend) ledger with installment tracking, and a zero-sign-up group bill splitter.
  • Multi-Tier Security Vault: Fortified by an 8-character master encryption password, a 4-digit quick launch PIN, and hardware-backed Android BiometricPrompt (fingerprint and 3D facial unlock).
  • Reactive State Machine: Clean Architecture with Kotlin StateFlow and Coroutines, ensuring 60fps fluidity across all Material 3 Jetpack Compose screens.

Technical Specifications

Kotlin Jetpack Compose (Material 3) Room DB (SQLite) Coroutines & StateFlow Android BiometricPrompt Clean Architecture (MVVM)

Key Learnings & Takeaways

Designing for offline-first demands rigorous SQLite schema migrations, transaction safety during multi-person expense splits, and handling edge states in biometric hardware without crashing fallback PIN authentication paths.

B2B Logistics // Solo Founder Startup

GatiSetu

370-km NH-48 Gujarat Freight Corridor · Deadhead Reduction · Driver PWA

The Industry Bottleneck

The 370-kilometer National Highway 48 corridor traversing Vapi, Surat, Ankleshwar, Bharuch, Vadodara, and Ahmedabad is India's dense industrial manufacturing artery. Every single day, thousands of commercial trucks deliver industrial consignments and then return completely empty ("khali fero" or deadhead), bleeding fuel and driver earnings. Concurrently, small-and-medium GIDC factories face severe spot-freight shortages and opaque broker markups.

The GatiSetu Solution

As a solo founder, I designed and built GatiSetu to directly match verified empty returning trucks with factory outbound freight along exact return coordinates.

  • Multilingual Driver PWA: Progressive Web App localized in Gujarati, Hindi, and English. Employs large touch targets and a 4-digit PIN authentication so non-tech-savvy drivers can log trips in seconds.
  • Factory Shipper Portal: Self-serve portal for GIDC dispatch managers to post outbound loads, preview verified vehicle specs, and lock transparent corridor rates.
  • Trip Profit Logbook: Visualizes outbound versus return freight revenue, revealing net profit gains to fleet operators.
  • WhatsApp Live Updates: Automated trip status and tracking links dispatched to dispatch desks without requiring proprietary app downloads.
  • Vehicle Verification & Multi-Tenancy: Complete organization-level data isolation and vehicle RC verification.

Tech Stack

Solo Founder & Architect Progressive Web App (PWA) Multi-Tenant Architecture WhatsApp Business API Webhooks High-Density Route Matching

Key Founder & Engineering Learnings

Field testing on NH-48 taught me that user adoption in logistics depends heavily on zero friction: eliminating long onboarding forms in favor of WhatsApp alerts and single-tap vernacular screens made the difference between driver rejection and genuine daily usage.
